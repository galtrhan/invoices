# Privacy Policy for Invoices

**Last updated:** July 29, 2026

Invoices is a desktop application that creates client invoices. It runs entirely on your device and does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers.

## Data storage

All data you enter into the application — company details, client information, invoice line items, logos, and preferences — is stored **locally** on your device in a SQLite database and configuration files under `%APPDATA%/invoices/`. No data is ever sent to us or any third party.

## Network

The application does not make network requests. It does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or analytics services.

## Third-party sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because no data leaves your device.

## Your control

You can delete all application data at any time by uninstalling the application or manually removing the data directory listed above.

## Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue at the project repository.
